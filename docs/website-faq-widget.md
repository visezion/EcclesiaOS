# FAQ widget

In Website Studio, open a reusable section and add **FAQs** to a column. Edit its heading and each question and answer inside the widget. Use **Add question** to create more rows, the arrow buttons to reorder them, or **Remove** to delete a row. Save the section and assign it to the pages where the FAQ should appear.

**First answer** controls whether the first row starts expanded. **Expand behavior** controls whether one or several answers may be open at once. The public widget uses accessible native disclosure elements, works without JavaScript, and follows the site's light or dark theme. Empty questions or answers are omitted from the public page.
